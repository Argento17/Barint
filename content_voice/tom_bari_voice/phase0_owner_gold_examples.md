# SYNTHESIS — what makes the owner's Hebrew read native (8 gold examples)

## A. The owner's native toolkit (positive patterns — the gate's "good" target)
- **Connectors that replace staccato:** `מדובר ב…`, `יחד עם זאת`, `כי` (causal),
  `במהותו`, `בגדול` (colloquial "basically"), `אומנם… אבל` (concession), `ביחס ל…`
  (comparative, grounded in the shelf), `כך ש…`.
- **Idiom over calque:** `כל קשר בין X ל-Y מקרי בהחלט` instead of a bare `X, לא Y`.
- **Structure:** opens with a calm orienting sentence (category principle or
  familiar-product context) → critique → **clean stop.** Numbers live inside full
  sentences. Verdicts are fair/balanced ("לא הכי מהונדס… אבל…"), not punitive.
- **The fix for the weak closer is usually to STOP earlier**, not to write a better
  punchline. Owner repeatedly deletes the live copy's stretched/recommendation-tail
  closers (#6 "למי שמחפש… כאן כאן", #1-protein "זה הטוב במדף מהונדס, לא מזון חזק").

## B. Anti-over-flagging (do NOT treat these as tells)
- `אשר` — owner uses it naturally (Ex.3 "מרגרינה אשר ירודה"). Not a tell.
- Short fragment closers — can be *earned* (Ex.2 "לייט זה לא."). T3 is the
  *dangling-`גם` / incoherent* fragment, not every fragment.
- Mild formal register (`הינה`, `מצריכה`, `ירודים`) — fine when it reads right.

## C. ⚠️ The gold is VOICE gold, not TERM gold
The owner's drafts repeatedly use **"תוספי תזונה"** (= dietary supplements) where
**"תוספי מזון"** (food additives) is meant — a `5_banned_phrases` violation
(Ex.2–5). Calibrate the naturalness judge on the owner's *flow/phrasing*; the term +
claim firewalls still run on top. The gold examples are NOT a license to adopt
"תוספי תזונה" or to skip any existing gate.

## D. ★ The central finding + owner ruling (2026-06-22)

The **live (mediocre) copy chases "punch"** — staccato fragments, calqued metaphors,
signature pivots, "(!)", `X, לא Y` closers — producing translationese *by trying too
hard to sound "Tom-punchy."* The owner's gold reads more natural. **But the owner
explicitly rejects "calm" as the target:** *"AI does not treat 'calm' nicely — it
becomes too neutral that it doesn't write anything. Should be somewhere in between."*

**THE TARGET REGISTER (owner ruling):** *opinionated substance delivered in natural
connected Hebrew.* Not punch-performance (translationese), not neutral-bland (says
nothing). The owner's gold proves both halves coexist: sharp verdicts/idioms
(`כל קשר בין X ל-Y מקרי בהחלט`, `לייט זה לא.`, `המתיקות מצריכה זהירות`) carried in
**connected natural prose** (`יחד עם זאת`, `כי`, `מדובר ב…`). The wit lives in the
**ideas and a few earned sharp lines**, not in fragmented syntax used as default texture.

### TWO failure modes — the gate must catch both
| Mode | What it looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **F1 — Translationese-punch** | staccato calques, `X, לא Y`, calqued metaphors, "(!)" as default, trying-too-hard | the current live copy / fingerprint over-emphasis |
| **F2 — Neutral-bland** | no verdict, no stance, hedge-only, generic, "says nothing" | the over-correction if you just tell the AI "be calm" |

A line is excellent only when it is **F1-clean AND F2-clean**: natural connected
Hebrew *with* a clear stance. Calm-but-empty fails just as hard as punchy-but-calqued.

### Fingerprint implication (recalibration, not deletion)
`2_voice_fingerprint.md` keeps its **stance/verdict commitment** (don't go neutral —
this is the F2 guard, and the existing "de-escalation is never the default" / "punch
on the egregious" rules protect against blandness). What changes: the **default
texture** moves from staccato/fragments/"(!)" to natural connected prose; punch moves
(fragments, "(!)", compressed pivots) become **seasoning used sparingly and only when
earned**, not the baseline. Recalibration target = the in-between register above.
Status: **owner-ruled; ready to drive Phase 1 (two-axis gate) + a fingerprint pass.**
