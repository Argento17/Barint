Can GLP-1 medications help older adults lose weight safely? What are the risks of muscle loss, frailty and malnutrition as we age?

In this episode of Aging Forward, Dr. Christina Chen sits down with Mayo Clinic obesity specialist Dr. Andres Acosta to discuss how GLP-1 medications like Ozempic® and Wegovy® affect older adults, appetite, metabolism and healthy aging.

Topics discussed include:
• How GLP-1 medications work
• Weight loss after age 60
• Muscle loss and sarcopenia
• Benefits for diabetes and heart health
• Side effects and safety concerns
• Nutrition, protein and exercise
• Long-term use of GLP-1s
• Healthy aging and longevity
