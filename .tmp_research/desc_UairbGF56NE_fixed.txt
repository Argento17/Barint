GLP-1 weight loss drugs like Ozempic, Wegovy and Zepbound are everywhere — but what do we actually know after a year of widespread use?

In this episode of Health Matters, Mayo Clinic dietitian Tara Schmidt joins us to break down the latest research, real-world challenges and emerging questions around GLP-1 medications.

From rising popularity to ongoing concerns about side effects, cost and long-term use, the conversation around these drugs is evolving fast.

In this episode, you’ll learn:
• How GLP-1 medications work for weight loss and diabetes
• The latest on side effects, including mental health concerns
• Why 50–75% of people stop taking GLP-1s within a year
• What’s changing with insurance coverage, cost and access
• The risks of compounded (non-FDA-approved) GLP-1 drugs
• What’s next: oral GLP-1s, new treatments and future research

Whether you’re considering GLP-1s or already taking them, this episode offers a clear, evidence-based look at the benefits, risks and unknowns.
