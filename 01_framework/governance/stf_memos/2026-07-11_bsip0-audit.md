   retailer/schema provenance declares separator semantics; else `NULL + FLAG`. Plausibility may
   reject/flag, never silently select. (Note: already-**published** values stay untouched — the
   builders already resolved e.g. 1628 mg correctly; this governs the parser/replay going forward,
   nothing on the live site changes.)
