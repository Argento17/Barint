# P116 / C3 — Bari Hebrew comparison-copy quality challenge (route: C3)

You are an INDEPENDENT Hebrew food-editorial critic. Do NOT produce product data, scores, or nutrition facts. Critique writing only. Return evidence-based judgement, not data.

Context: Two Bari comparison pages were generated by the same engine and data pipeline. One scored 8/10 with the owner, one scored 3/10. The data quality is the same — the gap is the WRITING.

## The 8/10 page (cookies / biscuits) — hero
"אם ממילא תיקחו ביסקוויט עם הקפה — הנה אילו מהם מרעים פחות."

## The 3/10 page (cakes) — examples the owner explicitly REJECTED
- Hero: "149 מוצרים נסרקו — אף אחד לא הגיע ל-A."
- Row verdict (brownie cake): "39 גרם סוכר ל-100 גרם, 12 גרם שומן רווי. שני הגורמים עיקריים בציון הנמוך."
- Row verdict (baked cheesecake): "עוגת גבינה אפויה עם 10 גרם חלבון ל-100 גרם — גבוה בחלבון לקטגוריה. רשימת חומרים עם 14 תוספי מרקם ומייצבים."

Owner's verdict on the 3/10 copy: "we are back to writing the nutritional information on the product" — the row verdicts just recite grams; the hero is a stat-header, not a hook. The numbers already appear in the table columns, so repeating them in the verdict adds nothing.

## Deliver, concretely (this is the whole task)
1. The 4–6 editorial rules that separate the 8/10 voice from the 3/10 voice. Be specific about what a one-to-two-line row verdict should DO (the interpretation / the catch / the "so what") INSTEAD of reciting grams that are already in the columns.
2. Rewrite the TWO rejected row verdicts above to the 8/10 standard — insight-first, a hook or a tension, the interpretation not the readout, still honest and grounded (you may keep at most ONE number if it genuinely carries the insight). Hebrew.
3. Three alternative HEROES (Hebrew) for an indulgent cakes shelf where nothing scored above C (no A, no B) — in the voice of the 8/10 example: a hook, not "X products scanned."
4. One sentence: the single most common failure mode to forbid in the copy spec.

Keep it tight and usable as a content brief.
