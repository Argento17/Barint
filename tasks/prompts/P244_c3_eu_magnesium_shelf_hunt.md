# P244 / EU magnesium real-shelf hunt (route: C3)

You are the outside-the-family research lane. Gather REAL EU magnesium-primary supplement products
for a worldwide benchmark. Evidence only — you do not build code or score anything. Every product
needs a source URL. Do NOT use Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) for anything.

## Goal
There is no central EU supplement database (Directive 2002/46/EC — national regulation). Assemble a
DOCUMENTED, PARTIAL sample of real, currently-marketed EU magnesium-primary products (exclude
multivitamins where magnesium is incidental). Anchor to national notification registries where
possible — Italy's Ministero della Salute *registro integratori* (salute.gov.it), France's Compl'Alim,
Belgium — plus current EU retailer/brand pages.

## For EACH product report
- brand + product name + country/market
- chemical form(s) (citrate, oxide, bisglycinate, blend…)
- compound mg and/or **elemental magnesium mg** per daily dose (convert if only compound given:
  oxide ~60%, citrate anhydrous ~16% / nonahydrate ~12%, bisglycinate ~14%, malate ~15.5%; show the math)
- the on-label claim (energy/fatigue, muscle, sleep…)
- price + currency + retailer + capture date (note: EU price is for the EU shelf record only; it will
  NOT be compared to other countries)
- source URL (registry entry or retailer page)

Aim for ~8–15 products across ≥2 member states. Quality + traceability over volume.

## Also report
- The distribution you can derive (median & ~90th-pct elemental mg, form mix) — explicitly labeled
  "indicative, partial" with the sample size.
- Which national registries you could actually access vs. which were blocked/PDF-only.

## Return (to tasks/returns/P244_return.md)
A product table with the fields above, the indicative distribution, the access/limitation notes, and
a source list with URLs. Flag any number you could not verify as "not found" rather than guessing.
