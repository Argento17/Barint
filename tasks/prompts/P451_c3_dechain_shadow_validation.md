# P451 — Independent challenge: is the de-chain + fixed confidence-gate shadow genuinely deploy-safe, or are we fooling ourselves?

You are C3, the independent challenge lane. Evidence-based red-team only; NO code, NO data production. Default to skepticism — Bari acts on your honest pushback. You have challenged this program before (P398/P399/P450) and were right to.

## What is being validated
Bari is "drifting from NOVA" (TASK-395): replacing a crude NOVA cap with (a) a continuous processing signal (`BARI_DECHAIN_V1`) and (b) an ingredient-confidence gate (`BARI_INGCONF_V1`) that withholds/caps grades when ingredient data is thin. Both flags now live on the ONE canonical engine, default OFF, proven byte-identical when off (53/53). A whole-corpus shadow ran on the 7 categories that reproduce their published baseline (373 products), comparing published / baseline-flags-off / candidate-flags-on.

## The chain of events to judge
1. **Shadow v2** found the de-chain PROCESSING signal sane/safe (small +3..+6 nudges, 0 clean-label inversions, 0 invariant violations) BUT the confidence gate demoted whole-food category WINNERS (plain milk/cheese/brined A→C@64.9) — all 12 large movers. Root cause: the gate classified ANY label without stated percentages as "low confidence," conflating "simple whole food, nothing to break down" (real high confidence) with "complex processed, proportions hidden" (real low confidence).
2. **Fix** (D6 spec + D7 co-sign): a general 3-gate SIMPLE-FOOD test — depth-0 ingredient count ≤5 + no contested ingredient (20-token lexicon + E-number regex) + no real sub-composite → treat as high-confidence (no cap); else cap at B(64.9). The none-tier withhold (zero ingredients → no grade) and the de-chain engine were untouched.
3. **Shadow v3** (post-fix): large movers >15 fell 12→2; new dominance inversions 93→72; whole-food winners restored 10/11; ingconf invariants 0 violations (0 none-with-grade, 0 low-at-S/A); clean-label refined-sugar top-3 = 0; global delta mean +0.09 / median 0 / stdev 3.55. The 2 residual large movers are claimed to be NOT gate errors: (a) a cottage cheese whose SCRAPED ingredient field was polluted with nutrition-panel+disclaimer text (phantom ingredient count >5) — a source-data artifact; (b) a 14-ingredient industrial bread correctly capped (has emulsifier E481 + acids). Claimed: no real 6-7-ingredient clean whole-food exists in the corpus, and 0 honey edge cases.

## Challenge these, hard
1. **The fix vs metric-shopping:** the gate was changed AFTER the shadow showed it demoting good products. Is the SIMPLE-FOOD test a principled fix, or did we just tune the gate until the embarrassing movers disappeared? What would distinguish the two? Is "count ≤5 + no contested token" a real proxy for "composition is unambiguous," or a convenient threshold?
2. **The residual-as-data-artifact claim:** is it intellectually honest to set aside the cottage-cheese demotion as a "scrape artifact," or is that explaining away a real failure? If the scraper pollutes ingredient fields, how many OTHER products in the live shelf have silently-polluted ingredient data that the gate (or the score) is misreading without anyone noticing? Does this undermine trust in the whole 373-product result?
3. **Generalization:** the validation is on 7 reproducible categories (mostly dairy/cheese/cookies/bread). The 5 unrepaired categories include snacks, granola, cereals — exactly the complex mixed whole+refined products where the confidence gate matters MOST. Does a clean result on the simpler-label majority tell us anything about how this behaves on the hard categories, or is the easy two-thirds masking the real test?
4. **The de-chain processing signal itself:** "small +3..+6 nudges, median 0" — is "it barely changes anything" actually evidence it WORKS (de-chaining was supposed to fix real NOVA inversions like the Petit-Beurre/Chokita case), or evidence it's a no-op that doesn't deliver the promised correction? Where's the proof it fixes the inversions it was built to fix?
5. **Coarse safety metric:** the dominance/inversion count uses a 4-axis proxy and the builder calls 72 "new inversions" mostly artifacts of the metric. Is dismissing 72 acceptable, or a red flag that the safety check is too weak to trust?
6. **Bottom line:** would you authorize this candidate (de-chain + fixed gate) to proceed toward an owner deploy decision on the reproducible majority — as a staged first wave — or do you counsel NOT deploying any of it until the hard categories (snacks/granola/cereals) are repaired and shadowed too? What is the single biggest remaining risk, and the one piece of evidence that would most change your verdict?
