### Numbers-kept justification (31/31 — why this category keeps more numbers than most fan-out categories)
Hard cheeses is a **near-monolithic B-cluster category** (26 of 31 products score exactly 67.0/B; only 1 A and 4 C). The category's own `categoryCaveat` (untouched, page-level copy) states the honest finding: fat-tech-aware scoring treats saturated fat as a natural dairy-matrix trait, so almost the entire shelf clusters at B, and the *only* things that move a product within or out of that cluster are sodium, ingredient-list length/cleanliness, and fat tier. Given that, the products' own sodium and fat numbers **are** the story in nearly every row — this is the category-honest equivalent of the brined-cheeses "4/47 shelf extremes" exception, generalized to a shelf where the number *is* the differentiator for almost every product, not just the extremes. Every number cited is directly sourced from `expansion.nutrition` and rank-checked against the full 31-product corpus (section c). No number restates a value already implied by the grade (i.e., no "which is why it's a B" — that framing is exactly what was purged).
