## 2. Corrected version of the six baseline signals (as they should actually read)

1. **US DGA 2025–2030 — CORRECTED & ENACTED.** *Final* guidelines were **released 2026-01-07** by USDA/HHS (not draft/advisory-stage). They emphasize protein (population target 1.2–1.6 g/kg/day), full-fat dairy, and explicitly advise avoiding "highly processed foods"; seed/vegetable oils are effectively dropped in favor of olive oil/butter/tallow. **Caveat:** they diverge from the 2024 DGAC Scientific Report, and parts (saturated fat, protein emphasis) are scientifically Contested. US policy context only — not Israeli authority, not a Bari scoring basis.

2. **US food-dye / UPF regulation — SPLIT, PARTIALLY OVERSTATED.** FD&C **Red No. 3 revocation is REAL and ENACTED** (announced 2025-01-15, FR 2025-01-16; food compliance by 2027-01-15) — but via the Delaney *animal-carcinogenicity* clause, with FDA explicitly stating **no evidence of human cancer risk** (do not present as proof of human harm). California **AB 2244 "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified" is a PROPOSED BILL (introduced 2026-03-24), NOT law** — must not be framed as enacted or as precedent.

3. **Protein dominance / protein-washing — REAL TREND, SOFTEN THE RISK TERM.** Protein is a genuinely dominant front-of-pack claim (well-evidenced commercially). "Protein-washing" is a credible, consistently-reported *marketing-commentary* framing — keep it, but label it as a marketing/health-halo observation, **not** a clinical or regulatory finding.

4. **GLP-1 reshaping consumption — REAL, CONTEXT ONLY.** Consumption shift and "GLP-1 friendly" labels are real and rising; the load-bearing fact is that **"GLP-1 friendly" is an undefined, unregulated marketing claim**. US market context; not yet evidenced on Israeli shelves.

5. **"Glucose Goddess" misinformation — REAL CONTROVERSY, ATTRIBUTION CORRECTED.** It is a genuine, professionally-contested misinformation case. **CORRECTION:** it is **not a Channel 4 *Dispatches* debunking** — Channel 4 *aired the show* (*Eat Smart: Secrets of the Glucose Goddess*, 2025); the **debunking came from the Freedom Food Alliance open letter (June 2025) and clinicians/dietitians.** Classify strictly as media/misinformation, never scientific.

6. **ZOE Processed Food Risk Scale — REAL, OBSERVE-DON'T-ADOPT.** A real commercial competitor methodology (3 axes: energy-intake rate, hyperpalatability, non-culinary additives; launched March 2025). Conflicted/limited validation. **Describe for differentiation; do not adopt or copy.**

- **Signal 3 (Protein-washing):** actionable in **content + research only**. Strongest fit to Bari's anti-deception mission; pair with Israeli scrape-data characterization before any consumer copy.

- **SOFTEN Signal 2's "ban/phase-out" framing.** Red 3 is one enacted dye action *under an animal-carcinogenicity clause with no human-harm finding*; AB 2244 is *not law*. Do not present either as proof of human harm or as established UPF regulation.
- **SOFTEN "protein-washing" (Signal 3)** from an implied scientific risk to a marketing/health-halo observation.
- **SOFTEN the DGA framing (Signal 1)** to note it is enacted *but* diverges from its own scientific advisory committee and is Contested in parts; it is US, not Israeli, authority.
- **No fully UNVERIFIED rows.** Every signal tied to ≥1 real, dated, primary/authoritative source. The previously-implied "DGA still in draft" and "Red 3 = proven human harm" and "Channel 4 debunked it" sub-claims are the items corrected/rejected above.

## 6. Overall verdict: **PASS WITH FIXES**

All six baseline signals are anchored to real, dated, verifiable primary or authoritative sources — none is fabricated and none is fully unverifiable, so this is not a FAIL. However, three material overstatements must be corrected before any implementation task is opened: (1) the DGA 2025–2030 is **enacted (Jan 7 2026), not draft**, but diverges from its own scientific committee and carries no Israeli authority; (2) the dye/UPF signal conflates an **enacted single-dye action that explicitly found no human-cancer risk (Red 3)** with a **non-enacted California bill (AB 2244)** and must not be framed as a sweeping "ban" or proof of harm; and (3) the Glucose Goddess item is **not a Channel 4 Dispatches debunking** — the debunking is an external clinician coalition. With those fixes applied, the register is usable: actionable items are confined to content/research, scoring scope is correctly empty, and frozen invariants are untouched.
